Beef Burrito

Try out this everyday Beef Burrito recipe using simple, but very tasty ingredients and nicely seasoned beef mince. If you're into prepping meals, this is a great choice for something to chuck in the freezer that you can pull out and reheat super quick.

Description

Calling this burrito an everyday burrito couldn't be more appropriate, it's to cook up initially and since they're so easy to freeze/reheat you can have them ready to go at any time. It's such a simple recipe that you can absolutely add your own flair to it - heat things up with some extra spice or vary the veggies with some fajita style roast peppers!

Since these burritos are likely to be frozen as suggested, they don't have a lot going on in terms of sauces to avoid things getting too soggy (no-one wants a soggy burrito!). That said, I highly recommend putting tofaether some simple dipping options to go with these that really tie it all together - an easy guacamole or some spicy mayo work great.

Ingredients

You're going to need the following ingredients to work alongside the recipe below:

  • 500g lean beef mince
  • 6 - 8 large soft flour tortillas
  • 3 cups of cooked rice
  • 3 cups of shredded cabbage (for freezing) or lettuce (for fresh serve)
  • 2 garlice cloves (chopped)
  • 1 cup of corn
  • 1 cup of black beans
  • 1 red onion (finely chopped)
  • 150g shredded cheese
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1/2 tbsp olive oil
  • A selection of spices/seasonings:
    • Onion powder
    • Black pepper
    • Cayenne pepper
    • Dried cumin powder

Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a skillet over high heat. Add the garlic and 1/2 of the chopped onion, cook for 2 minutes until the onion begins to brown. Add the beef and cook until it turns from red to brown, breaking up as you go.
  2. Add seasonings (1 tsp onion powder, 2tsp cumin powder, 1/4tsp black pepper, 1/4tsp cayenne pepper) to the beef and cook for a further minute. Then add the tomato paste and 3tbsp of water, cooking for around 3 minutes until the water has mostly evaporated. Set the cooked beef aside for 10 minutes to cool off slightly - if it's ripping hot it'll make the burrito soggy.
  3. Heat your tortillas in a pan over medium heat, only a few seconds on each side is needed or follow packet instrucionts for microwaving.
  4. Place warm tortilla on a work surface and scoop 1/3 cup of your rice just below the centre. Add 1/3 cup beef mince on top of this followed by lettuce/cabbage, corn, black beans, tomato, chopped onion and cheese.
  5. Fold up the bottom of the tortilla to cover the fillings and then fold the edges in. Roll all of this up tighlty and wrap in foil.

From here they're good to be served or you can freeze them. When it comes to reheating, best options are to heat in a pan over medium high heat while still wrapped in the foil for a few minutes on each side until. Alternatively, bake in the oven/air fryer at 180C for about 20 minutes.
