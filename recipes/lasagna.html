Lasagna

Who doesn't love a good Lasagna?! A real comfort food that brings back memories of sitting round a table back home enjoying Mum's cooking. This home-made recipe will wrap you up in a warm hug and leave reaching for that next slice!

Description

That moment when you cut into the golden bubbly cheesy top and you serve up a piping hot, oozy, meaty slice of lasagna…. That's a little bit of food heaven, right there.

PS Also happens to be one of the nicest food gifts you can make for someone. Freezes 100% perfect, and is a huge step up from the usual simple casseroles. Right? 😉

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 medium carrot (diced)
  • 1 celery stick (diced)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1kg beef mince
  • 800g crushed tomato
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 1 cup pinot noir red wine
  • 3 beef bouillon cubes
  • Selection of seasonings:
    • Bay leaves
    • Dired thyme
    • Dried oregano
    • Worcestershire sauce
    • Sugar
    • Salt
    • Black pepper
  • 350g lasagna sheets
  • 1 1/2 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Bechemel sauce (homemade or a jar is fine)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180C.
  2. Heat oil in a large heavy based pot over medium heat. Add garlic, onion, celery and carrots. Cook for 10 minutes until softened and sweet - they should not brown (if they do, turn heat down).
  3. Add beef, turn heat up and cook the beef, breaking it up as you go.
  4. Once the beef has all turned brown, add the remaining Ragu ingredients EXCEPT the sugar.
  5. Stir then adjust the heat so it is bubbling very gently. Place the lid on and cook for 1.5 - 2 hours, stirring every now and then, then remove the lid and simmer for 30 minutes.
  6. The ragu is ready when the meat is really tender and the sauce has thickened and is rich. Adjust salt and pepper to taste, and add sugar if required.
  7. Using a large baking dish (recommend around 33 x 22 x 7cm), mear a bit of Ragu on the base, then cover with lasagna sheets. Tear/break sheets to fit.
  8. Spread over 2 1/2 cups of Ragu (enough to cover sheets), then drizzle over 1 cup of bechemel sauce.
  9. Top with lasagna sheets (Note 7). Spread with another 2 1/2 cups of Ragu, then 1 cup of bechemel sauce. Top with lasagna sheets then repeat 1 more time.
  10. Top with a 4th layer of lasagna sheets, then pour over the remaining bechemel sauce. Sprinkle with Mozzarella, then bake for 25 minutes or until golden and bubbling.
